# Framing: card table, top-down (not yet built)

A tabletop seen from above: the table is the stage, everything on it is the cast.

- **The table is quiet:** one material (felt, wood, cloth), dim and low in saturation, no pattern
  louder than a weave. It obeys the stage rules exactly.
- **Cards, tokens and boards are the cast:** ink outlines, flat fills, the one shadow tone with
  halftone. Drop shadows on the table are flat hard shapes in a darker stage tone, not soft blurs.
- **Characters** appear as portraits on the table's edge or in panels, not on the table.
- **Focus:** the active zone of the table stays at full strength; inactive zones dim by lowering
  their value, not by blurring.

**Expected pitfalls:** many cards at once make a busy screen; cap what's face-up and stack the rest.
Halftone shadows under dozens of cards turn into noise; keep halftone for cards being moved.
