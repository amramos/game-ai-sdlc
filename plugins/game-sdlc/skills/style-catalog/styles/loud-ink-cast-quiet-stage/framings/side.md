# Framing: side view (not yet built)

A side-scrolling or side-on scene; the stage becomes parallax layers.

- **Parallax:** 3–4 stage layers, each dimmer and lower in contrast the farther back it is. The
  layer the characters walk on is the only stage layer with outlines, and they are thin.
- **Characters** keep the full cast treatment; animation uses held poses and smears in the shadow
  tone rather than many in-betweens, which suits the flat, inked look.
- **Interface** panels stay in screen space, tilted labels anchored to what they name.

**Expected pitfalls:** a fast-moving camera smears the halftone into shimmer (keep halftone out of
anything that scrolls quickly); a bright foreground layer breaks the focus rule.
